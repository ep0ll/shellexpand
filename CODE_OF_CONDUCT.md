# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
project experience for everyone, regardless of experience, identity,
background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be kind and constructive. Assume good intent, discuss technical ideas rather
than people, accept feedback gracefully, and help keep discussions focused
and inclusive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks,
doxxing, threats, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in
project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
contact mechanism described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or GitHub's private
maintainer contact options. Reports will be handled discreetly and reviewed
fairly.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments and contributions that
violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary.
